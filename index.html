ABOUT

Hi, I'm Ma Patricia.

I'm a remote content creator and video editor. I started out making carousels, statics and ads for social media. Now most of my work is AI content and video: AI models and campaign video for a streetwear brand, plus vlogs and reels for a lash educator and a brand studio. For the brand studio I also run social media day to day: planning, posting, engaging with the community and reporting on what worked.

I like owning the whole process, from the idea to the AI generation, the edit and the final export in every size a platform needs. I also keep an eye on what's performing, so each new round of content builds on what already worked.
